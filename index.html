About Our School

Building knowledge, character and opportunities for the future.

Kanyigo Secondary School

Kanyigo Secondary School is located in the Kagera Region of Tanzania. The school was officially established in January 1985 through the efforts of the Kanyigo Development Association (KADEA).

The school provides secondary education from Form One to Form Six, with Ordinary Level and Advanced Level studies. Its educational mission is guided by the motto “Education for Development.”

The school aims to help learners gain academic knowledge, practical skills, discipline and values that prepare them for further education and responsible participation in society.

  • Ordinary Level and Advanced Level education
  • Education for both boys and girls
  • Day and boarding arrangements
  • Academic learning and practical activities
  • Sports, games and student development
